# Problem statement

## The problem

Calling names in a class of 60 takes 5 to 8 minutes every lecture. Paper sheets get signed for
absent friends. Many colleges moved to a QR code on the projector, which is fast, but a fixed code
has an obvious hole: one student takes a photo and posts it in the class group, and the whole group
"attends" from the hostel.

Teachers know this happens. What they lack is evidence. They cannot accuse a student on a hunch,
so the cheating goes unchecked, and attendance numbers stop meaning anything.

## Who has it

- College and school teachers who must report attendance (often tied to exam eligibility)
- Training institutes and coaching centres with large batches
- Workshop and event organisers who issue certificates based on attendance

## What we promise, and what we do not

| We promise | We do not claim |
|---|---|
| Attendance in under a minute for a full class | To stop every possible form of cheating |
| Clear flags with a reason the teacher can read | To decide punishment; a person reviews every flag |
| Blocking of the common tricks: shared screenshots, old codes, one phone for many | Location tracking or face recognition (we chose not to) |
| A design that keeps very little personal data | Replacing the institute's official records system |
