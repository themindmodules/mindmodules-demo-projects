# Team roles (3-4 people)

| Role | Owns | Hands off to |
|---|---|---|
| **Hardware / firmware** | Sensor module, ESP32 code, wiring and safety | Data lead (CSV rows) |
| **Data / analytics** | `analyze.py`, thresholds, injected-fault tests | Frontend (data.js) |
| **Frontend / demo** | Dashboard, polish, backup video | Pitch lead |
| **Pitch / story** | Slides, script, judge Q&A, interviews | Everyone (rehearsal) |

Team of 3: the pitch lead also does frontend. Team of 2: the hardware person drops hardware and the
project runs as the software demo with a clearly stated hardware plan.

## Daily check-in questions (every 3 hours)

1. What works end to end right now?
2. What is blocking you?
3. What can we cut?
