# Problem statement

## The problem

Small shops, clinics, cold-storage corners and homes see their electricity only as a monthly bill.
Appliances fail slowly (a compressor that stops cycling, a loose connection that heats up) and the
mains supply itself misbehaves (sags, surges). The owner finds out after the food is spoiled or the
equipment is burnt.

A smart meter gives totals. A plug-in energy monitor gives a number on a screen. Neither tells you
**"your fridge has been running non-stop for 90 minutes, which is not normal"**.

## Who has it

- Small shop owners with a fridge or freezer holding stock
- Homes with elderly people living alone (unusual usage patterns matter)
- Small workshops with expensive equipment on unstable supply

(You will need to confirm these with a few real interviews before pitching. Do two or three
quick conversations at the hackathon venue or with a local shop and quote what they say.)

## What we promise, and what we do not

| We promise | We do not claim |
|---|---|
| Plain-language alerts about abnormal load patterns | Exact fault diagnosis |
| Voltage sag / surge logging with timestamps | Replacing a certified protection device |
| A low-cost, open design anyone can build | Metrology-grade billing accuracy |
